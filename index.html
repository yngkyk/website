My name is Young-Kyung Kim. I am a second-year PhD student in political science at the Massechusetts Institute of Technology, studying American politics and political methodology.

I am interested in political behavior and communication; specifically, how the expression of emotion mobilizes group identities. To better understand these, I use computational and mixed methods to probe different forms of political messaging.
